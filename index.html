1 Introduction

Welcome to Play N Learn ("we", "our", or "the app"), an early childhood educational mobile application designed to assist toddlers and young children in learning colors, shapes, matching, and problem-solving through interactive mini-games.

We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of young users and providing peace of mind to parents and legal guardians. This Privacy Policy details how the app handles information, adheres to child privacy regulations, and maintains compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Because Play N Learn is designed for children, safeguarding their privacy is our highest priority. We comply strictly with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy:

  • No Personal Information Requested: The app does not require account creation, logins, user profiles, or any forms asking for children's names, birthdates, addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Public Sharing: There are no chat rooms, social networking features, leaderboards with public handles, or capabilities for children to share text, photos, or voice recordings.
  • No Tracking or Profiling: We do not collect persistent identifiers to build behavioral profiles of child users.
  • Parental Inquiries: If a parent or guardian believes their child has somehow provided personal information to us, they can contact us immediately at info.kalavay@gmail.com, and we will promptly ensure it is erased.

4 Local Data Storage & Offline Operation

The educational activities and games in Play N Learn run locally on your Android device. Any state saved by the app (such as level completions, audio toggles, high scores, or preferred game modes) is managed using Android's built-in isolated sandbox storage (e.g., Room database / SharedPreferences).

This data belongs entirely to the user, stays within the device, and can be cleared at any time via Android system settings.

5 Device Permissions Explained

Play N Learn operates with minimal permissions. It only requests the bare minimum necessary for platform operation:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Declared for standard Google Play platform compatibility and basic connectivity checks. No personal user data is ever transmitted.
  • No Dangerous Runtime Permissions: The app does not request sensitive permissions such as ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO, READ_CONTACTS, or broad storage access.

6 Third-Party Services & SDKs

The app utilizes trusted, standard platform services provided by Google. These service providers handle data according to their own privacy standards and comply with child-directed policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, updates, and core Android platform libraries. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase / Crashlytics (Platform Utilities): If enabled, used purely for crash diagnostics, performance monitoring, and app stability without collecting user identities.

7 Advertising Policy

In strict adherence to the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not serve personalized, behavioral, or interest-based advertising to children.
  • We do not track user behavior across other apps or websites.
  • Any educational content or updates presented comply fully with COPPA and age-appropriate guidelines.

8 Data Retention & User Rights (Right to Delete)

Because we do not store personal information on remote servers, there is no remote account or database record to purge.

Parents and users retain complete control over all game data stored on the device. To erase all data:

  1. Open device Settings > Apps & Notifications > Play N Learn.
  2. Select Storage & Cache.
  3. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data).
  4. Alternatively, uninstalling the application completely deletes all locally stored data.
